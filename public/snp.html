The Experience Exchange × Shenandoah National Park Trust

Bringing the next generation of storytellers, volunteers and park lovers to Shenandoah.

What we've built

The Experience Exchange (TEE) is a student-run outdoor, environmental and experiential storytelling magazine. It started at Washington and Lee and is now growing to campuses across the country.

40+students on our creative team
$5,000in ad revenue from our first edition
~90%of advertisers renewed for Edition 2
81%of readers would recommend TEE

A real print magazine

About 75 pages per edition, two editions a year, distributed on campus, at the W&L Bookstore, local cafés and partner retailers.

A growing student network

Writers, photographers, designers and Campus Correspondents at schools across the country, plus a podcast in the works.

Partners who come back

Local businesses like Walkabout Outfitter and Lex Running Shop advertise with us, and nearly all of them renewed.

The idea: TEE as the Trust's official student media partner

Our readers are exactly who the Trust's next chapter depends on: students who love the outdoors, care about sustainability (86% of our readers factor it into what they buy) and want to get involved.

A Weekend in Shenandoah

Words and photos by TEE students
About an hour from campus, the Blue Ridge opens up into 200,000 acres of hollows, waterfalls and overlooks. We spent a weekend on the trails, met the people working to protect them, and learned how students can be part of the park's next chapter. Day one started before sunrise on Skyline Drive, with fog still sitting in the valleys below. By noon we were knee-deep in a creek with a Trailblazers crew. The Trust's youth program pairs young people with real stewardship work, from trail repair to native planting.
