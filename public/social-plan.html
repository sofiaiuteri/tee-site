Instagram and TikTok · Lily Nannini and Bryn Hoppy

Goal

Grow Instagram from about 1,300 to 2,000 followers by the end of the year, with strong engagement (comments, shares, saves). Our audience size is what sponsors pay for.

Content

You have the freedom to find and create content that you think fits TEE. If Sofia sends something specific to post, like an advertiser feature or an event, that goes first. Beyond that, the creative direction is yours, so feel free to try new ideas.

Daily growth (Bryn)

Spend a few minutes each day following relevant accounts and unfollowing ones that don't follow back, to keep a healthy follower ratio. Keep advertisers, partners and team members followed.

Best practices

Hook in 2 seconds

Videos 15 to 45 seconds, captions on screen.

Make it shareable

Shares drive the most new followers. Tips and spot lists work well.

Mix formats

Carousels get saves and comments. Reels reach new people.

No TikTok watermark

Export from CapCut or your camera roll. Instagram limits watermarked Reels.

Prompt replies

End captions with a one-word question. Reply to every comment.

Collabs

1 or 2 a month with advertisers, student magazines or similar-sized creators.
