Bryn's daily growth routine

What makes posts do well

Hook in 2 seconds

The algorithms reward people watching to the end and rewatching. Keep videos 15 to 45 seconds with captions on screen.

Make it sendable

Sends are the biggest way new people find us on Instagram. Think "send this to who you're going to the beach with," tips and spot lists.

Carousels + Reels

Carousels get the most saves and comments. Reels reach the most new people. That's why we do both.

No TikTok watermark

Export from CapCut or your camera roll, not the TikTok download. Instagram shows watermarked Reels to fewer people.

Ask a one-word question

End captions with something easy to answer, like "beach or mountains?" Reply to comments, and answer good ones with a video.

Collabs

Collab posts with advertisers, other student magazines and creators our size show up on both accounts and reach new people. Aim for 1 or 2 a month.
