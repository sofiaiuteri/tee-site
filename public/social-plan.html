Daily growth (Bryn)

Spend a few minutes each day following relevant accounts and unfollowing ones that don't follow back, to keep a healthy follower ratio. Keep advertisers, partners and team members followed.

Best practices

Hook in 2 seconds

Videos 15 to 45 seconds, captions on screen.

Make it shareable

Shares drive the most new followers. Tips and spot lists work well.

Mix formats

Carousels get saves and comments. Reels reach new people.

No TikTok watermark

Export from CapCut or your camera roll. Instagram limits watermarked Reels.

Prompt replies

End captions with a one-word question. Reply to every comment.

Collabs

1 or 2 a month with advertisers, student magazines or similar-sized creators.
