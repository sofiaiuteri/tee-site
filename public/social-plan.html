TEE Social Plan: TikTok + Instagram

For Bryn · ongoing, starting the week of Oct 19

The goal: grow both accounts to the point where brands pay us to post. That means growing followers, and getting comments, shares and saves. Sponsors look at both.

What the algorithms reward: people watching to the end (and rewatching), and people sending your post to a friend. Sends are the biggest way new people find you on Instagram. So every post should either hook people in the first 2 seconds or be something worth sending. [1]

Why this schedule: that's 4 posts a week on Instagram and 3 on TikTok. Buffer's data from millions of posts shows the biggest growth jump comes from posting 3 to 5 times a week on Instagram and 2 to 5 on TikTok. Posting more helps a little, but less than staying consistent. [2] [3]

Post each video to both apps, but export it from CapCut or your camera roll, not the TikTok download. Instagram has said it shows Reels with a TikTok watermark to fewer people. [4] Keep videos 15 to 45 seconds, with captions on screen. Short videos that people rewatch do better than long ones they skip.

Collabs (1 or 2 a month)

Why: in a study of over 500,000 posts, Collab posts beat regular posts, and Reels got the biggest boost. The catch is that it works best with partners whose followers aren't already ours. [5]

  • Sponsors Instagram Collab posts with Walkabout Outfitter and Lex Running Shop. The post shows up on both accounts, so we reach their followers too. (Sofia sets these up and films in Lexington.)
  • Other media Other student magazines and outdoor or art creators around our size. Trade a Collab post, or stitch and duet each other on TikTok.
  • Our people Feature a correspondent's photo or story and tag them. They'll share it with their followers.

Getting more interaction

Carousels get the most likes, comments and saves per view of any format, and Reels reach the most new people. That's why the plan has both. [6]

  • Make posts people want to send: "send this to who you're going to the beach with," useful tips, spot lists.
  • Reply to comments, and answer good questions with a video reply. It's an easy extra post.
  • End captions with a question people can answer in one word, for example "beach or mountains?"
  • Monthly challenge, #TEEoutside: people post their outdoor spot or nature art and tag us, and we feature the best ones.
  • Use Story polls and question boxes every week.
